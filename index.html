Applied Scientist II · Amazon Robotics

Hritam Basak.

Teaching robots to see, reason, and act in the real world.

I develop whole-body control and manipulation policies for humanoid robots at Amazon Robotics. Working with the Fauna Robotics and Industrial Robotics groups, I turn language goals into navigation and manipulation behaviors through agentic navigation and skill orchestration.

Download CV Scholar GitHub LinkedIn

My research spans 3D vision & robotics, vision-language-action models, generative AI, and multimodal learning (Vision + X), with applications in medical image analysis. I also mentor interns working on dexterous manipulation and robust grasping in cluttered environments.

I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at Stony Brook University in May 2026, advised by Prof. Zhaozheng Yin. Previously: two research internships at Amazon, data science at Tata Digital / Tata Neu, and research visits at ETH Zurich and Sorbonne University. I earned my B.E. in Electrical Engineering from Jadavpur University.

ECCV 2026

Fixed Reality, Diffused Possibility: Disentangling Stochastic and Deterministic Latent for Cluttered Grasping

Hritam Basak, Zhaozheng Yin

Project ↗ Code ↗

We propose a split-latent hierarchical diffusion framework that disentangles deterministic geometric priors (fixed latent) from stochastic scene-level context (diffused latent) for reliable affordance-grasp prediction in cluttered, partially observed environments. A Global Conditional Diffusion produces the stochastic latent from RGB context, a Local Conditional Diffusion predicts dense point-wise affordance and grasp fields, and a Harmonic Grasp Field enforces smooth, obstacle-aware grasp manifolds, yielding physically consistent and uncertainty-aware grasp generation.
